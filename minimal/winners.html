Minimal Circuit Winners

Circuits that learn in their life which colour is poison, under 10% mismatch in every part, shot and membrane noise, and eye pulses at random moments. Since the night of 2026-10-20 the job also asks that a creature keep eating: at most 5% of lives may go their last third without food. The earlier winners failed that, spinning on the spot for ever in about a fifth of their lives once they had eaten what was near. HFT_E1_23p adds a hunger and passes.

How they work

  1. The clock spins it. M1 fires on its own about 185 times per 1,000 ticks in both circuits (140 to 250 as the mismatch varies). One thruster alone pushes and turns, so with nothing in view the creature circles and its eyes sweep all round.
  2. Food sends it straight. When an eye sees the colour the bit says is food, its pulses kick M0, the second thruster fires too, and the creature drives at the block.
  3. Poison slides past. The other colour kicks nothing, so the creature keeps circling and the block leaves its view.
  4. The bit. m is a capacitor with no leak. Each read takes m as its input and an eye as its gate: one passes S0's pulses when m is above its step, the other S1's when m is below its step. At birth m sits between the two steps (0.30, between −0.18 and +0.50, in N_E1_23p; 0, between −0.48 and +0.49, in N_E1_32p), so both colours kick M0 and the creature approaches everything.
  5. Hunger (HFT_E1_23p only). A meal drains H to about −2 V. It relaxes back toward its rest, just below 0 V, over 1,000 to 3,000 ticks, and its noise carries it over 0. While H is above 0 the clock's spikes also kick M0, so both thrusters fire together and the creature runs straight: 55 to 73 units in 500 ticks, against 1 to 5 while it spins.
  6. Learning, from food only. T rests below 0 V, so the writes' gates are shut. A good taste lifts T to between 1.2 and 3 V for a moment and opens both writes; each moves m its own way in proportion to how recently its eye fired. The sensor's own decay (τ 8 to 17 circuit ticks, 2 to 4 ticks of the world) is the memory of what was just seen. A bad taste pushes T further down and opens nothing.

What differs: the clock

N_E1_23p resets its clock with a large slow current (gain −28, τ 291). That is nearly all its power: with the reset zeroed, power falls from 7.2 to 1.1, though the clock then stops working. N_E1_32p resets gently (gain −1.25, τ 22) and filters its fast feedback (τ 0.7), for 4.8 in all.

A gated element is two transistors in series. A filtered input adds a resistor and a capacitor. The values are as tuned; every part is then off by about 10% at birth, drawn from the seed. These are found values, not designed ones.

Still open

  • Reversal. If the poison colour swaps halfway through a life, every circuit here keeps eating the old food, now poison. They learn only from food: a bad taste shuts the writes' gates.
  • Pruning leans on the noise. Judged at the nominal noise alone, pruning swaps mechanisms for noise, which costs no parts. HFT_E1_23p pruned to 22 parts does the job at the nominal noise, but at half the noise 19% of its lives strand.
