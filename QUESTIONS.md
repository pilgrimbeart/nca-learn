# Open questions

What we'd like to know and haven't tested, roughly most valuable first. A question raised mid-quest is parked here
rather than chased; one answered leaves, its answer recorded in the notebook. Each: the question, who raised it and
when, why it matters, and the experiment that would answer it.

## The sheet brain (`NOTEBOOK.md`)

- **Self-optimisation from local signals**: every unit tuning its own time constants and thresholds from its own
  signals, with no fixed wait or threshold tuned to one sheet (AGENTS.md: no hidden size), so that the sheet adjusts
  itself to the body it finds itself in. The project's real goal.
- **Learning faster**: the sensors learn from taste alone, and meals are few (about 1.5 per 10 s), so in a quarter of
  lives the sensors have not both come right by 100 s (README, Result). How much is the brain's, and how much the
  body's? A brain told the answer from birth sets the limit to compare against.
- **Fewer shared numbers** (28 now; the lizard's 16 also carried direction, which ours must build).

## The body (`NOTEBOOK.md`, The body)

- **Would evolving the body pay more than evolving the brain?** (the user, 2026-10-30) The limits found so far are
  information: sparse meals to learn from, and a far block seen as about one flash as the gaze sweeps past. Sharper or
  wider eyes might buy more than any brain. A body change that adds information differs from one that does the
  brain's work (credit assignment, filtering, timing: never, AGENTS.md); and the body's organs are the user's ground
  rules, so any such experiment is the user's decision.
